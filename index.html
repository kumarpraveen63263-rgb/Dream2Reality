PARTICIPATION GUIDELINES

Eligibility Criteria

Review the mandatory qualifications and submission requirements before registering your team.

Eligible Cohorts

Students from pre-final and final year of engineering programs are eligible to participate.

Team Composition

Maximum of 4 members / team. Mandatory rule: One member should be a girl student.

Challenge Scope

The ideas should address industrial or societal challenges through interdisciplinary and cross-functional approaches.

Innovation Required

The idea must be innovative and present a distinct creative solution rather than a replica of existing systems.

Commercial Potential & Feasibility

The idea should demonstrate potential for commercialisation along with technical feasibility, market relevance, affordability, and practical implementation potential.

TRL Alignment

Ideas should preferably be aligned with Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 1 to 3 (concept formulation, proof of concept, or analytical/lab validation).
